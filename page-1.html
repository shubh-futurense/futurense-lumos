Why This Degree Is a Game-Changer in the Next 3 Years?

According to a joint NASSCOM–BCG 2024 report, India’s AI workforce is on the cusp of a massive expansion, with 2.3 million AI jobs expected to be created by 2027. But here’s the catch—only 1.2 million skilled professionals are projected to be available to fill them.

Moreover, a PwC global report projects that AI will add $15.7 trillion to the global economy by 2030, making AI the single biggest commercial opportunity in today’s digital economy.

The Shift Is Already Underway

And it’s not just about more jobs—it’s about different jobs. A Gartner 2024 study forecasts a 180% spike in GenAI-specific roles like prompt engineers and AI ethicists by 2026. In fact, 92% of AI job listings today demand skills like Python and TensorFlow, underscoring the importance of industry-grade, deployable knowledge—not just textbook theory.

It’s about building what comes next—with IIT backing you.

About IIT Jodhpur

The Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur, established in 2008 in Rajasthan, is one of India’s fastest-growing IITs. Ranked 27th in the NIRF 2025 Engineering Rankings, it is steadily building a strong reputation in AI, Data Science, and emerging technologies. The institute’s School of Artificial Intelligence & Data Science brings together pioneers working on areas like generative AI, robotics, and computational neuroscience. With interdisciplinary programs, advanced research centers, and strong industry collaborations, IIT Jodhpur is shaping innovation and driving future-ready talent.

About Program Directors

Dr. Suman Dhaka

Assistant Professor, Psychology in the School of Liberal Arts at IIT Jodhpur

Dr. Suman Dhaka is an Assistant Professor of Psychology in the School of Liberal Arts at IIT Jodhpur and an affiliated faculty member in the School of Artificial Intelligence & Data Science. She specializes in cognitive neuroscience, neuropsychology, and cognitive psychology. Her research focuses on the multidisciplinary study of affect regulation, interactions between sleep and emotion, and their influence on human behavior and decision-making. She previously served as Assistant Professor in the Department of Humanities and Social Sciences at NIT Rourkela before joining IIT Jodhpur in December 2020. Dr. Dhaka earned her integrated B.Tech–M.Tech in Cognitive Neuroscience from the University of Rajasthan and completed her Ph.D. in Cognitive Psychology at IIT Guwahati. Her work employs EEG, sleep studies, brain stimulation, and computational modeling to investigate the neural bases of emotion–cognition interactions.

Dr. Dhaka leads funded research projects and has published her work in peer-reviewed journals. She maintains active collaborations with national and international researchers. Her awards and fellowships include DAAD, DST-CSRI, DST-IAS, DBT-IPLS, FENS–IBRO PERC, and the Brain Prize travel grant.

Frequently Asked Questions

We know you might have some questions before getting started in our platform

Does IIT Jodhpur also offer a B.S/B.Sc in Applied AI and Data Science on campus?

There are other BTech and B.S/B.Sc programs available on the campus. However, a program specifically in Applied AI and Data Science is a first and not offered on campus.

Will those enrolled in the program have access to IIT Jodhpur’s campus facilities?

There will be optional campus immersions. These immersions will be for 3-5 days at the IIT campus, every year. There will be additional fees for immersion, accommodation, and food. Other than this, students can always visit the campus and look around.

What are the eligibility criteria for the degree program?

Students must have completed their 12th grade or equivalent education with a minimum of 60% and Mathematics as a mandatory subject.

What is the total tuition for the program?

The total tuition comprises Rs. 1,32,000 per year for four years,
Convocation Fee: ₹3,000 (One-time; to be paid directly to IIT Jodhpur; not included in program fees)
Immersion Fees: ₹4,500 – ₹7,500 (for 3–5 days, including accommodation and food; to be paid directly to IIT Jodhpur; not included in program fees)

Is the program available to students currently in class 12th or equivalent?

The primary eligibility criterion is a passing grade of at least 60% in your 12th or equivalent exams. Additionally, it's essential that Mathematics be a mandatory subject in your 12th-grade curriculum.

Is there an entrance exam for admission?

Yes, Students who have not cleared JEE (Mains) will need to sit for our qualifier exam to meet the eligibility requirements for this program. Upon successfully passing the qualifier exam, students can proceed with their application process seamlessly.

Will I be provided with a physical degree from IIT Jodhpur?

Yes, a physical degree will be provided. This degree will be from IIT Jodhpur.

I am a B. Com Graduate / Lawyer / BSc. Graduate / Mechanical engineer and do not have any knowledge of coding. Does this course cover the basics of coding before progressing to advanced levels?

Indeed, this course begins from the foundational level, making it an ideal choice for individuals seeking to pursue an add-on degree or aiming to enhance their skills while obtaining a degree in Applied AI.
